🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Last updated: November 2026

At RAB Community Group, we take the privacy of our station managers and staff very seriously.

1. What Data We Collect

When you register as a station, we collect your Name, Cellphone Number, and Location so that we can contact you and manage your station.

2. How We Use It

We use your phone number for SMS or WhatsApp updates regarding your station, and your location to help customers find you.

3. Messages and Voice Notes

Any messages or voice notes you post in the Community Chat are only visible to registered RAB staff. We do not share these conversations with the public or third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security

Your passwords are encrypted (hashed) using industry-standard security. Our login portal is protected against brute-force hacker attacks. We never store your password in plain text.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account at any time. To do so, contact the Administrator.
